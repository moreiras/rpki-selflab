Delegated RPKI —

In delegated mode, the holder runs their own certificate authority and keeps their own private key. This registry only issues the certificate that grants the resources to the holder's CA, and hosts the repository where the signed objects get published. These are two independent steps, each with its own XML exchange.

1 CA delegation RFC 6492 · Up-Down protocol

Paste the holder CA's identity (child request). The registry replies with the parent's identity and the service address (parent response), which needs to be brought back to the holder's CA.

2 Publication service RFC 8181

Paste the publisher request (publisher request). The registry replies with the repository's address and its RRDP publication point (repository response).
